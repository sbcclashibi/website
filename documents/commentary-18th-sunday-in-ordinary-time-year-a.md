# 18TH SUNDAY IN ORDINARY TIME YEAR A
Theme: The Messianic Banquet

---

#### 1st Reading: Isaiah, chapter 55: 1-3

Like the Hebrews in the Babylonian exile, this scripture is meant for all the faithful who feel dejected, and without hope. God is about to set us free, and supply everything we need. He will do so through an everlasting covenant.

#### 2nd Reading: Romans, chapter 8: 35, 37-39

St Paul observes that there is nothing in all creation that will separate the believing Christian from the love of God: that love which comes to us through Christ, our Lord.

#### Gospel Reading: Matthew, chapter 14: 13-21

The initial reaction of the disciples was to send the hungry crowd away. Jesus’ solution to the problem, however, is both practical and symbolic, thus setting the tone for the way our Priests look up to heaven, bless the Eucharist and feed the faithful.
